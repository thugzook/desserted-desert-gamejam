Desserted Desert Pattern Editor

10 measures of 4/4, 16th-note grid. Notes mark when the attack arrives at the player — the beat they must dodge or parry on. Export and drop the JSON into dodge-guy-gamejam/patterns/.
Click or drag to paint notes, click again to erase. Space plays/stops. The striped ANY row keeps the beat but lets the game pick a random attack direction. Your work autosaves in this browser.
